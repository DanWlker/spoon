Spoon panels

Where each panel is on the two pages, its number, and where the focus keys take you. Tab switches between the pages. A number focuses its panel directly; Ctrl-h/j/k/l (or Ctrl and the arrows) focus the panel beside; Alt-h/j/k/l (or Alt and the arrows) move the border of the focused panel, which can be dragged too. See the keymap for every key.

Windows over the pages

B opens the blame of a file over everything: [1] Commit, the commit it is shown as of, at the top, and [2] Blame, the lines, below; Ctrl-k/j move between them, and in the lines Ctrl-h/l between the commit column and the text. T opens the timeline: [1] Commits on the left and [2] Diff beside them, Ctrl-h/l between them. In both, Alt-h/l moves the border after the commits. ? opens the keys list over the page, @ the command log.
